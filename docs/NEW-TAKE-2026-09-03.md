# FAVOUR — the new take

Written 2026-09-03 from live counters and a rendered look at the shipped app.
Not a ruling. Every number below was read from production Redis or measured in a
390×844 mobile viewport, not taken from a doc.

## 1. What people actually do

All-time event counts, against the 17,923 feed loads people performed anyway:

| count | share | what |
|---|---|---|
| 17,923 | 100% | looked at the board |
| **2,868** | **16.0%** | **REAL OR NOT verdicts** |
| 1,062 | 5.9% | signed in |
| 788 | 4.4% | submitted proof — DID a favour |
| 489 | 2.7% | opened the daily quest |
| 135 | 0.75% | voted in a poll |
| 93 | 0.52% | POSTED a favour |
| 45 | 0.25% | staked a prediction |
| 26 | 0.15% | opened a referral share |
| 4 | 0.02% | got real money |

Fourteen consecutive days to 2026-09-03: ~20 sign-ins/day, ~80 feed loads/day,
**0 or 1 favours done, 0 or 1 posted.** The marketplace is not decaying. It is
stopped. The peer-jury game out-performs it by 30×.

## 2. The finding that explains everything

**REAL OR NOT is fed by the marketplace, and the marketplace has stopped.**

A jury card needs a proof that is (a) attached to a completed favour and (b)
already passed by the AI verifier. Measured live:

- Judgeable pool: **28 proofs.**
- Newest one belongs to a task created **2026-07-24** — six weeks ago.
- A judge is capped at 20 verdicts/day, and the deck excludes proofs they have
  already ruled on. **A regular exhausts the entire pool in 1.4 days.**
- `jury_verdict` was **0 on both Sep 2 and Sep 3**, after running 9–74/day.

The one feature anyone loves is starved by the one nobody uses. That is the
product in a sentence, and no amount of new content on the board fixes it,
because the board is not what they came for.

## 3. The take: stop shipping a marketplace with a game attached

Run the **game**. Make the favour the *prompt*, not the job, and make the
marketplace the game's content pipeline.

The loop becomes **shoot → get judged → judge others**. Taking the photo is the
move, not the work.

**The one change that unblocks it:** a proof should enter the jury deck when it
is *submitted*, not when its task *completes and passes*. There are 50 tasks
carrying a proof image against 28 that qualify today — the pool nearly doubles on
day one and then grows with every submission instead of every completion. It also
removes the circularity: today the AI must pass a proof before humans may judge
it, which means the peer jury only ever sees what the model already approved. Let
humans see what the model rejected. That is a better game *and* a better eval.

## 4. What is genuinely unused (checked, not assumed)

MiniKit 2.0.3 and IDKit 4.1.2 are already installed. Haptics are already wired
across Feed (40 call sites), Polls, Predictions and JuryMode; share and pay
helpers exist. Do **not** re-add those.

Actually unused:

- **`shareContacts` / `getContacts`** — the World App contact picker. Zero call
  sites. Referral has 26 opens all-time; this is the only real social lever on
  the platform and it has never been touched.
- **Push notifications** — `WORLD_NOTIFICATION_API_KEY` is set and
  `lib/daily-notify.ts` is written, but it ships dark behind
  `DAILY_NOTIFY_ENFORCE`. A daily game with no notification is a daily game
  nobody returns to. "3 new proofs to judge" is the whole retention story.
- **World Chat** — one call site. XMTP still carries the messaging weight
  (there is a 
  `.db3` in the repo root).
- **Selfie Check** (IDKit-only, World ID 3.0, access-gated) — the missing rung
  between `device` and `orb` in `verification-tier.ts`. The cash gate is
  Orb-only, which is why `unlock_paid` is 4.
- **AgentKit / AgentBook** — `/api/agent/register` needs `ADMIN_SECRET`;
  `docs/AGENT-DOOR.md` says *"today only the FAVOUR operator can mint one."*
  Zero external agents ever registered was read as no demand; a door no outsider
  can open explains it at least as well.

## 5. On "it isn't pretty any more"

Rendered at a verified 390×844 mobile viewport. The layout is technically clean:
`scrollWidth 390`, **0 overflowing elements**. Nothing is broken. The problem is
emptier than that.

- **Two full-screen splashes before any content.** `/` shows a coach screen
  ("Pick a small task, do it, send proof, earn points", 4 dots, Get started),
  then a second screen ("Real tasks. Real people. Verified on-chain." +
  Continue). The second is **~14 words on an 844px screen** — roughly 70% empty
  white, with the section labels TASKS • POLLS • CAMPAIGNS in grey so light they
  read as disabled. The bottom nav is already rendered and highlighted behind
  both, so the app looks loaded and empty at the same time.
- **The profile is a wall of zeros** — seven `0`s on one screen, and the boldest
  element on it is `Buy · 30`, a points sink. The first thing a new user is
  invited to do is spend.
- **No colour anywhere.** DESIGN-SYSTEM.md restricts green to money and amber to
  points; with no money and no points, the result is grey text on white inside
  grey cards inside grey cards. The discipline is right and the outcome is a
  spreadsheet.

**The fix follows from §3, not from a restyle.** The first screen should be a
photo to judge. Cold-open into REAL OR NOT: one real photo, one real favour
description, swipe. It is the only surface with an audience, it needs no
account, no Orb, no money, and it explains the whole product in one gesture —
"is this real?" Onboarding copy is what you write when the first screen has
nothing to show.

## 6. Cheapest ordered path

1. Jury pool takes submitted proofs, not just AI-passed completed ones. One
   predicate in `lib/jury.ts`. Pool 28 → 50 immediately, growing after.
2. Cold-open into the jury. Delete both splash screens.
3. Flip `DAILY_NOTIFY_ENFORCE` and notify on judgeable-deck refills.
4. `shareContacts` on the invite.
5. Selfie Check as the middle assurance rung (needs beta access — email gate).
6. AgentKit as the agent door (needs Sandbox access — form gate).
